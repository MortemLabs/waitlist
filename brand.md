# Brand — Mortem

> Case File № EA-001 · Early Access · Rev 1.0 · 2026-05-04
>
> Ship. Bury. Repeat.

This site inherits Mortem's brand system from the main product. Keep the clinical tone, the ink-paper-signal palette, and the square, filed-document feel. The page may be more commercial than the app, but it should still read like a coroner wrote it.

## 1 · Voice

We don't hype. We file.

- Primary: `Ship. Bury. Repeat.`
- Product: `Every project gets a death certificate.`
- Early access: `Catch bad trading decisions before more capital gets buried.`

Do: name the cause, not the symptom; use finality verbs; keep the tone calm and exact.
Don't: use startup fluff, emoji, exclamation points, or generic SaaS clichés.

## 2 · Color

- `--ink`: `#0E0D0C`
- `--paper`: `#EDEEE9`
- `--signal`: `#DC2626`

Keep the 60 / 30 / 10 ratio. No gradients outside ink/paper/signal relationships, and no secondary accent color.

## 3 · Typography

- Display: Instrument Serif
- UI: Inter Tight
- Evidence: JetBrains Mono

## 4 · Layout

- No border radius.
- 8px grid.
- Card surfaces sit on `--ink-2` with borders, not shadows.
- Motion is clinical, not playful.
